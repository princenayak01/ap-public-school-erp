# A. P PUBLIC SCHOOL, JHANSI (CBSE)
## Parent & Guardian User Guide (Web & Android App)

### 1. Accessing Your Parent Portal
- **Website**: Navigate to `http://localhost:3000/login`
- **Android App**: Launch the **A. P PUBLIC SCHOOL** mobile application.
- **Login Credentials**: Enter your registered mobile number or email and password.

---

### 2. Switching Between Multiple Children
If you have more than one child enrolled at A. P PUBLIC SCHOOL:
- Click the child's name tag at the top of your dashboard (e.g. **Aarav Sharma (Class 4-A)** or **Ananya Sharma (Class 1-B)**).
- The portal instantly updates to show that child's specific attendance, tuition dues, and examination results.
- **Privacy Assurance**: Your account can only view your own verified children.

---

### 3. Paying School Fees Online
1. Click the **Tuition Fee Dues** card or **Pay Online via Razorpay** button.
2. Select your preferred payment method:
   - **UPI**: Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM
   - **Debit / Credit Cards**: Visa, Mastercard, RuPay
   - **Net Banking**: All major Indian banks
3. Upon successful transaction, an official PDF receipt with receipt number and QR signature is instantly available for download.

---

### 4. Viewing Attendance & Report Cards
- **Attendance Gauge**: Check your child's monthly and annual attendance percentage. A minimum of 75% is required by CBSE guidelines.
- **CBSE Report Cards**: Access Mid-Term and Annual examination marks, subject breakdown, overall percentage, and Principal remarks.
- **AI School Assistant**: Click the AI Assistant icon anytime to ask questions regarding school timings, fee deadlines, or CBSE grading.
