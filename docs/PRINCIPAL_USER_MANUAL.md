# A. P PUBLIC SCHOOL, JHANSI (CBSE)
## Principal & Administrator Operational Manual

### 1. Dashboard Overview
Upon logging in with your Principal credentials:
- **School Health KPIs**: View live student strength (Nursery to Class 8), today's attendance compliance, monthly fee collection, and outstanding dues.
- **Class-Wise Capacity**: Review interactive visual bars displaying student counts across all 11 classes.
- **Recent Admissions & Ledger**: Monitor recent admissions and digital payments in real time.

---

### 2. Student Admission & Promotion
1. **Admitting a Student**:
   - Go to **Students & Admissions** -> click **New Student Admission**.
   - Enter student personal details, assign Class and Section (A or B).
   - Enter Parent details (Father, Mother, contact phone, and login email).
   - Click **Confirm Admission**. A unique Admission Number (`APPS/2026/XXX`) is generated.
2. **Promoting Students**:
   - At the conclusion of the academic term, use the Promotion tool to advance eligible students to the next grade level in batch mode.

---

### 3. Fees Management & Collection
1. **Generating Monthly Invoices**:
   - On the 1st of every month, click **Fee Billing** -> **Generate Monthly Invoices**.
   - System calculates base tuition, applies individual concessions, and adds late fee rules.
2. **Collecting Offline Payments**:
   - Select invoice -> Click **Cash/UPI** -> Enter payment amount -> System issues official numbered receipt.
3. **Monitoring Online Payments**:
   - Payments made by parents via the mobile app or website Razorpay gateway are automatically reconciled in the audit ledger.

---

### 4. Examinations & CBSE Report Cards
1. **Setting up an Examination**:
   - Schedule Mid-Term or Annual examinations, define subjects, max marks (100) and pass marks (33).
2. **Generating Report Cards**:
   - Click **Generate Reports**. The system computes percentages and assigns official CBSE 9-point grades (`A1` to `E`).
   - Click **Download PDF** to export signed report cards for parent distribution.
