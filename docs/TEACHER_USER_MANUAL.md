# A. P PUBLIC SCHOOL, JHANSI (CBSE)
## Teacher & Academic Staff Operational Guide

### 1. Teacher Portal Login
- Access the portal using your employee email (e.g., `teacher.neha@appsjhansi.edu.in`) and password.
- View your assigned classes, subjects, and daily teaching schedule.

---

### 2. Marking Daily Class Attendance
1. Navigate to **Daily Attendance** -> select your assigned Grade (e.g. Class 4) and Section (A).
2. Select today's date.
3. Use the one-click action buttons:
   - **Mark All Present**: Quickly sets all students to Present.
   - Click individual student buttons to flag **Late** or **Absent** with optional remarks (e.g. "Medical Leave").
4. Click **Save Daily Attendance**. Parents of absent students automatically receive notification alerts.

---

### 3. Entering Examination Marks
1. Go to **Exams & CBSE Results**.
2. Select the examination (e.g., Mid-Term Examination 2026) and your assigned subject.
3. Enter marks obtained for each student (Maximum: 100).
4. System automatically checks for input errors (marks > max marks) and computes CBSE grades (`A1` to `E`).
5. Click **Save Marks**.
